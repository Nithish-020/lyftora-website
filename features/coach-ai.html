—— Honest answers

The questions you'd ask over coffee.

Is Coach AI a real trainer or a chatbot?+
It's a clinical engine that uses six expert role-systems — trainer, dietitian, sports medicine, clinical, behavioral, sport-specific. It refuses to plan without screening, calculates everything openly, and references physicians when something is out of scope. A chatbot generates output. Coach AI assembles a program.
What if I have a medical condition?+
Tell it. Thirteen conditions are handled with automatic plan modifications — diabetes (T1/T2), hypertension, heart disease, kidney disease, osteoporosis, PCOS, pregnancy, postpartum, recent surgery, eating-disorder history, severe obesity, back/disc issues, asthma, thyroid, arthritis. Each triggers specific rules (kidney disease caps protein at 0.6–0.8 g/kg, heart disease blocks HIIT, etc.).
Does it know Indian and regional foods?+
Yes — Indian, South Indian, North Indian, Middle Eastern, East Asian, Latin, Western, and Mixed cuisine databases. Plans are built from your real pantry, in your real cuisine, at your real budget tier.
Can it adapt my existing diet plan?+
Yes. If you're already on a plan you like, Coach AI reviews it for macro balance, calorie accuracy, and gaps — then refines it. It respects the work you've already done.
What if I'm pregnant or postpartum?+
Pregnancy triggers an OB-GYN referral — Coach AI will not program training for pregnant users without clearance. Postpartum < 6 months prioritizes pelvic floor and gradual reconditioning.
Does it work without internet?+
Yes. Plans, calculations, and history are stored locally. You can train, log, and review without a single bar of signal.
