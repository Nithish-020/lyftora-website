—— FAQ

The questions you'd ask over coffee.

Is Coach AI a real trainer or a chatbot?+
It's a clinical engine using six expert role-systems — trainer, dietitian, sports medicine, clinical, behavioral, sport. It refuses to plan without screening, calculates everything openly, and refers serious cases to physicians. A chatbot generates. Coach AI assembles.
What if I have a medical condition?+
Tell it. Thirteen conditions are handled with automatic plan modifications. Kidney disease caps protein at 0.6–0.8 g/kg. Heart disease blocks HIIT until a clearance flag. Pregnancy triggers OB-GYN referral. Each rule is visible in the plan. For clinical questions, reach us at support@lyftora.in.
Does it know Indian and regional foods?+
Yes — Indian, South Indian, North Indian, Middle Eastern, East Asian, Latin, Western, Mixed. Plans are built from your real pantry, in your real cuisine, at your real budget tier.
Can it adapt my existing diet plan?+
Yes. Share your current plan — Coach AI reviews it for macro balance, calorie accuracy, and gaps, then refines rather than replaces. It respects the work you've already put in.
What about pregnancy or postpartum?+
Pregnancy triggers an OB-GYN referral — no training programmed without clearance. Postpartum under six months prioritizes pelvic floor and gradual reconditioning.
Does it work without internet?+
Yes. Plans, calculations, and history live on your device. Train, log, and review without a single bar of signal.
